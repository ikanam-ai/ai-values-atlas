# Curation protocol

## Verification levels

| Status | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|
| discovered | Link and source-catalog provenance |
| catalogued | Stable title, canonical publication URL, year, and rough scope |
| verified | Methods checked in the primary work; study and axiology relations completed |
| audited | Code/data/model artifacts, versions, licenses, and reproducibility checked |

## Primary-source rule

Publisher pages, DOI records, ACL Anthology, OpenReview, arXiv, official project
pages, and official repositories are preferred. Search snippets and third-party
awesome lists are discovery sources only.

## Summaries

Store short original summaries, not copied abstracts. A summary should identify:

1. the construct;
2. the measurement interface;
3. the axiological representation;
4. the scoring method;
5. the main validation evidence;
6. the most important limitation.

## Review queue

Prioritize:

1. systematic reviews and foundational value theories;
2. datasets, benchmarks, and released measurement models;
3. highly cited or recent empirical value-profile studies;
4. cross-interface, behavioral, generative, and representational studies;
5. alignment methods and adjacent work.
